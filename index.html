About the Province

The Land of the
Great Hero

Named in honor of the Philippines' national hero, Dr. José Rizal, the Province of Rizal sits at the eastern doorstep of Metro Manila, bordered by the majestic Sierra Madre mountain range. This dynamic province blends urban accessibility with breathtaking natural scenery, making it a top destination for adventure seekers, history enthusiasts, and culture lovers alike.

Home to pristine waterfalls, rugged mountain trails, ancient petroglyphs, world-class art museums, and vibrant festivals, Rizal Province offers an unrivaled diversity of experiences — all just a short drive from the bustling capital.

Natural Wonders

From the dramatic limestone karsts of Masungi to the crystal-clear Tinipak River and the thundering Daranak Falls, Rizal boasts unmatched natural diversity.

🏛️

Rich Heritage

The Angono Petroglyphs, Pinto Art Museum, and centuries-old churches reveal the deep cultural roots and artistic soul of the province.

🎭

Vibrant Culture

Renowned as the "Arts Capital of the Philippines," Angono is home to world-famous artists and hosts the spectacular Higantes Festival each November.
